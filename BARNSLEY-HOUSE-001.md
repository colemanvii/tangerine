# Haven House 001 — Barnsley Gardens Pilot

## Purpose

Turn one Freeman-built home at Barnsley Gardens into the first real Haven home.

This is not a smart-home demo. It is a builder-to-homeowner continuity system: the house acquires a durable memory while it is being built, then Haven becomes the calm interface through which the owner understands and maintains that home.

**Freeman builds the house. Haven remembers it.**

## Pilot thesis

Premium homes contain enormous amounts of knowledge that become fragmented or disappear after construction: field decisions, concealed conditions, finish selections, equipment details, manuals, warranties, service contacts, and maintenance requirements.

Haven captures that knowledge at the moment it exists and turns it into a living home record.

At turnover, the homeowner does not receive a binder. They receive Haven.

## Phase 01 — Capture during construction

For House 001, collect:

- current plans and specifications
- room and finish schedule
- paint colors and product codes
- appliances, equipment models, and serial numbers
- HVAC, electrical, plumbing, water, generator, network, irrigation and security information
- photographs before insulation and drywall
- shutoff and panel locations
- material selections and substitutions
- architect, designer, superintendent, subcontractor and service contacts
- warranties and manuals
- meaningful field decisions and dates

Do not capture data merely because it exists. Capture information a future owner, builder, technician, or caretaker will actually need.

## Phase 02 — House model

The first data model should be deliberately small:

HOME
- rooms
- systems
- components
- documents
- field photographs
- decisions
- service events
- contacts
- maintenance tasks

Every physical component should be locatable in the house and capable of linking to its relevant documentation.

Example:

Barnsley House 001
→ Mechanical
→ Heat Pump 01
→ model / serial / install date
→ filter specification
→ warranty
→ manual
→ installer
→ service history
→ next maintenance

## Phase 03 — Homeowner surface

The primary interface answers four questions:

1. What is happening?
2. Are we okay?
3. What needs me?
4. What happens next?

Primary navigation:

HOME → SYSTEMS → SECURITY → HOUSEHOLD → RECORD

The daily surface stays sparse. The deep record exists when needed.

## Phase 04 — Wall surface

Test Haven on a dedicated tablet-sized display in a natural household transition point such as the mudroom, kitchen, owner entry, or home office.

The display should work from several feet away and should not demand attention.

Idle state:

HAVEN
Home is good.

No feed. No advertising. No engagement mechanics. No wall of smart-home controls.

## Phase 05 — Selective integrations

Only after the record and daily brief are useful on their own should Haven ingest live home state.

Start read-only and low-risk.

Potential categories:
- climate
- doors / gates
- water monitoring
- power / generator
- network
- environmental sensors
- weather

Haven should unify state without pretending to replace professionally designed security, fire, life-safety, or building-control systems.

## Freeman workflow

During construction, the Freeman team should not have to become software operators.

Haven should fit the work already happening:

FIELD PHOTO → TAG TO ROOM/SYSTEM → SAVE
DOCUMENT → ASSOCIATE → SAVE
SELECTION/DECISION → RECORD → SAVE
EQUIPMENT INSTALLED → IDENTIFY → SAVE

The long-term opportunity is for the Haven record to become a byproduct of building the home well.

## Definition of success

House 001 succeeds if:

- Freeman can populate the record without creating a burdensome parallel workflow.
- A homeowner can understand the current state of the home in roughly ten seconds.
- A homeowner can answer a specific house question without calling the builder.
- A service technician can find the relevant component information quickly.
- The record is useful even with zero live smart-home integrations.
- Gabe can imagine saying: **Every Freeman home comes with Haven.**

## Guardrail

Do not let House 001 become a generic home-automation dashboard.

The wedge is **memory + continuity + orientation**.

The home can become connected later. First, it must become known.
