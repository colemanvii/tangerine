# Tangerine — repository guidance

## Current mandate (16 November 2026)

**Tangerine is a small independent marketing and creative studio.** It offers marketing, advertising (including paid/social), PR and communications, brand, storytelling, creative direction, design, film, photography, illustration, typography, and related execution. Its real model is a handful of deep, long-term client relationships.

**Long-term games with long-term people. We make great stuff, put it out into the world, and let the work speak.**

Tangerine is **not currently** a media-news startup, newsletter platform, AI synthesis interface, TABLET app, consumer SaaS, home operating system, or broad client-acquisition agency. Those ideas are archival experiments. Do not resurrect them by inference.

## Before changing things

1. Read [README.md](README.md) for current studio definition and [IDENTITY.md](IDENTITY.md) for design studies.
2. Inspect current branch, existing PRs, deployment assumptions and relevant files.
3. Name the specific client deliverable and intended scope. Do not pull in unrelated experiments.
4. Favor focused changes on review branches. Verify before representing anything as live.
5. Keep the website spare: work, relationships, identity, contact. Resist service-bloat, noise, and gimmicks.

## Clients, prospects, rights, confidentiality

- This repository is **public**. Do not expose personal data, confidential correspondence, budgets, contracts, private creative briefs, unreleased photographs/artwork, customer information, credentials or source files without clear authorization.
- Joseph Martin, Kashus Culpepper, and Freeman are the working relationship focus. Public client naming, logos, photographs, specific campaigns and case studies require clearance before publication.
- Scout Motors is an **aspirational partnership, not an established client**. Never imply otherwise.
- Do not infer ownership, licensing rights, royalty terms, endorsement, releases, or permission to publish from a concept discussion.
- Preserve original art and authorship, and distinguish studies from approved client work.

## Website design direction

A beautiful, minimal studio website: clear typography, expansive cyber-white space, graphite, precise orange, fine rules, and first-rate editorial judgment. No rounded-card dashboards, excessive gradients, gratuitous motion, decorative complexity, or AI-forward product positioning. Industrial Study 004 and the original small fruit mark remain *identity studies*, not final licensed marks.

The homepage revision on `feature/tangerine-studio-clarity-2026-11-16` is a **review candidate**. Review partner-name permissions, links, responsive behavior, and deployment setup before merging or sharing publicly.

## Historical artifacts — preserve

`main` still includes earlier Haven HTML and JavaScript. Preserve those files and history unless a specific archival migration is authorized. `SYNTHESIS.md`, `INSTRUMENT.md`, `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and TABLET/news branches are earlier explorations; they do not define today's studio. Do not merge outdated experiments, redeploy them, or delete them just to simplify the visible repo.

## Definition of done

Make one clear thing, make it excellent, protect rights and relationships, test it, and make its status obvious. The creative work is the point.
