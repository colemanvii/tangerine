# Tangerine repository instructions

## Identity and product authority

Tangerine is an independent creative brand: **A place to keep what matters and return to it.** The digital archive is its first active product exploration, not the entire scope of the brand.

For digital product implementation, preserve the existing archive specification on `study/tangerine-wrapped-astra-001` and the immutable-source principles below. The newly approved **Tangerine personal instrument** direction is documented in `INSTRUMENT.md`; its bounded TABLET implementation study lives on `study/tablet-cyber-white-001`. This product direction does not authorize a deployment, merge, private-data import, or destruction of existing archive/legacy work. Do not interpret broader brand language as authorization to build, announce, or deploy unapproved projects.

## Privacy and continuity

- **The GitHub repository is public.** Do not publish private writing, photographs, personal records, conversation exports, secrets, client-confidential data, or unannounced strategic projects.
- A protected repository would not automatically make its hosted site private. Verify deployment visibility separately.
- Original writing and collected materials remain untouched. AI-generated metadata, analysis, and derived statistics must stay separate, sourced, and reversible.
- Do not fabricate usage hours, dates, analytics, or provenance. Chat timestamps are not screen-time telemetry.
- Unmigrated legacy task examples remain in `personal/index.html`; do not represent them as synchronized to Tangerine.
- Do not change Vercel deployment settings, production branches, repository visibility, or audience incidentally.
- Preserve historic files and branches. Migrate and cut over deliberately, with verification.

Do not assume a GitHub commit changed a production website.
