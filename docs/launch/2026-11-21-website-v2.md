# Website V2 — a living index

November 21, 2026 · branch `claude/tangerine-website-v2-tsnpvz`

## What changed

- **Homepage** (`/`): "We build brands." over an index of four relationships. Each entry shows its number, since, status, artifact count, disciplines and a strip of the actual work. Then Studio, an archive log, and Contact (Instagram @ex.tangerina).
- **Four archives:** `/freeman-general-contracting/`, `/joseph-martin/`, `/kashus-culpepper/` and `/scout-motors/`.
  - Each has a title block, discipline filter, chapters of artifacts in composed spreads, and a full-screen viewer (previous/next, counter, Escape, swipe, deep links `#artifact-id`).
  - Each ends with the next relationship.
- **Identity:** the approved script wordmark (byte-identical PNG from the previous homepage) is the mark. Paper `#f8f5ed`, graphite `#191714`, orange `#df4900`, with `#bf3d00` for small orange text so it passes 4.5:1. Archivo (variable width) and Instrument Serif, both OFL, self-hosted.
- **Content model:** `website/content/*.json` → `website/tools/build.mjs` writes static HTML, so there is no build step on Vercel. `tools/media.py` makes WebP derivatives and records provenance. `tools/og.mjs` makes social cards. `tools/check.mjs` is the smoke test. See `website/README.md`.
- **Previous homepage:** archived unchanged in `archive/script-homepage-2026-11-18/` (blob recorded in its README). The unreferenced smartscreen files are moved beside it.
- **`vercel.json`:**
  - keeps the `/screen` redirects;
  - adds `trailingSlash`, short temporary redirects (`/freeman`, `/kashus`, `/scout`), security headers, and cache headers.

## Verification (local)

Served `website/` with `python3 -m http.server` and drove it with Playwright (Chromium) at 1440×900, iPad Pro 11 and iPhone 13:

- `tools/check.mjs`: no console errors, no failed or 4xx requests, no horizontal scroll, one h1 per page, alt on every image, every image decodes. Viewer open, next, Escape and deep link work, and the filter works on every size.
- axe-core (WCAG 2.1 AA + best practice): no violations on any page or in the viewer.
- Initial transfer: about 0.8 MB on the homepage (desktop and phone) and about 0.5 MB on Freeman. Images are lazy-loaded and video uses `preload="none"`.

**Not verified from this environment:** the Vercel preview, production, and the public domain. This session's network cannot reach `*.vercel.app` or `ex-tangerina.com`.
