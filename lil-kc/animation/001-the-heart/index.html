Model sheet · measured from the master

What makes him him

Proportions

A / B
Head, afro included, is about three fifths of his height (556 of 950 px). The body is the smaller part.
C
The afro is 2.1 times as wide as his face (597 px against 281 px), lumpy all round, never a circle.
D
The face is a small window low in the hair: forehead under the bandanna, cheek pushed forward, pointed little chin.
E
The near eye is a third of the face's height. The far eye is slimmer and open at its lower rim. They do not match, and should not.
F
Each bandanna tail trails about one face-width behind the knot, with a notched end.

Face studies for this shot

Eyes are redrawn from his own traced eye outline: the pupil swings round inside it and the closed lid follows the lower rim. Brows, nose, mouth and ear keep their traced shapes.

Silhouette

  • Hair, tails, dagger and kicking foot all read in solid black.
  • The heart sits inside the outline, behind the body line of action.

Palette sampled from the master

Median colour of each region. His gi is bare paper, not a separate white.

Ink

  • Edges are slightly dry and uneven. Hair and belt are solid ink masses, not outlines.
  • Colour is flat with paper grain. No shading, no highlights except the nick at the top of the heart.

Held constant in every key

  • Head-to-body ratio, afro lumps, eye spacing and asymmetry.
  • Dagger arm stays brave and extended while the heart arm does the tender work.
  • Mouth stays a tiny "o". No grin, no frown, no angry brows.
Provenance

How these drawings were made

  • Key 01 is a measured tracing of the master: ink centre-lines with their brush width, solid ink masses and flat colour shapes, re-inked by code. No pixels of the master are reused.
  • Keys 02 to 06 start from that tracing. The eyes are redrawn, brows and mouth are repositioned, the heart-and-hand is its own layer so it can be pulled in, and the body, head, afro, bandanna tails, dagger arm and kicking leg are bent by small hand-set amounts.
  • That makes these traced-and-adjusted studies, not freehand drawings. They stay very close to the master on purpose. If you want any key pushed further, or drawn by hand and dropped in as a replacement PNG, the pipeline accepts that.
  • Files: transparent PNGs at 1536 × 1024 in storyboard/, rebuilt by project/build.py from project/keys.py.

What I need from you before animating

  • Does every key still look like him? Check the face at Face zoom, over the master.
  • Is the acting right: glance down to the heart, blink, see the danger, pull the heart in, resolve?
  • How far should the heart travel in Protection, and should the poses be broader or stay this subtle?
