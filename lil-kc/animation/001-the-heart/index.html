Model sheet · measured from the master

What makes him him

Proportions

A / B
Head, afro included, is about three fifths of his height (556 of 950 px). The body is the smaller part.
C
The afro is 2.1 times as wide as his face (597 px against 281 px), lumpy all round, never a circle.
D
The face is a small window low in the hair: forehead under the bandanna, cheek pushed forward, pointed little chin.
E
The near eye is a third of the face's height. The far eye is slimmer and open at its lower rim. They do not match, and should not.
F
Each bandanna tail trails about one face-width behind the knot, with a notched end.

Faces in this test

Eyes are redrawn on his own traced eye outline: the pupil swings round inside it and the lid comes down over it. Brows, nose, mouth and ear keep their traced shapes.

Silhouette

  • Hair, tails, dagger and kicking foot all read in solid black.
  • The heart sits inside the outline, behind the body line of action.

Palette sampled from the master

Median colour of each region. His gi is bare paper, not a separate white.

Ink

  • Edges are slightly dry and uneven. Hair and belt are solid ink masses, not outlines.
  • Colour is flat with paper grain. No shading, no highlights except the nick at the top of the heart.

Held constant in every key

  • Head-to-body ratio, afro lumps, eye spacing and asymmetry.
  • Dagger arm stays brave and extended while the heart arm does the tender work.
  • Mouth stays a tiny "o". No grin, no frown, no angry brows.
Provenance

How this test was made

  • Drawing 1 is the measured tracing of the master, re-inked by code. No pixels of the master are reused.
  • Newly drawn for this test: the squeezed heart and the fist round it, the closed and half-closed lids, and the eyes looking down at the heart. The lids and pupils are built on his own traced eye outlines.
  • Still adjusted tracing: body, head, limbs, afro and bandanna. Acting is keyed by hand; the afro and tails follow late, and the tails carry a travelling wave.
  • Every drawing is re-inked with fresh edge and grain, so the line boils slightly the way redrawn frames do.
  • Files: frames/frame-0001.png to frame-0036.png with timing.json; exports in lil-kc/exports/ (transparent WebP, MP4 on paper).

What to judge

  • Does the squeeze feel protective and like him? The heart and fist there are my drawing, not the master's.
  • Are the half-closed lids too heavy? They can read sleepy or stern for a frame.
  • Is the bounce and bandanna the right size, or should it be calmer or bigger?
