# Tangerine

**A place to keep what matters and return to it.**

Tangerine is an independent creative brand exploring the relationship between memory, design, technology, and everyday life.

We make places for attention: digital tools, considered objects, publications, and experiences that give important things room to last.

Our work begins with a simple conviction: **what matters deserves to be kept well.**

## Principles

- **Keep the original.** Authorship and provenance matter. A record should never be silently rewritten.
- **Make room for attention.** Less noise, more meaning. Technology should clarify rather than demand.
- **Return to what matters.** An archive is useful when it brings something valuable back into view.
- **Care about the object.** Typography, material, proportion, and behavior are part of the work.
- **Build for a long life.** Prefer useful, enduring things over novelty for its own sake.

**This repository is public.** Do not commit personal archives, private writing, ChatGPT exports, credentials, identifying records, or confidential project information.
