# Tangerine

**An independent creative studio.** Marketing, advertising, PR, brand, storytelling, creative direction, design, illustration, typography, and production.

Tangerine is a small creative company built around a few enduring client relationships. We work closely with people we believe in, make good things, put them into the world, and let the work speak.

**Long-term games with long-term people.**

## What we do

- Brand strategy, identity, creative direction and design
- Marketing and advertising, including social media marketing and paid campaigns
- Public relations, communications, partnerships and campaign strategy
- Storytelling, editorial, copywriting, filmmaking and photography
- Illustration, typography and other creative production

These capabilities are a toolkit, **not** a requirement to turn the website into an exhaustive services catalog. The work and relationships should lead.

This is a public repository. Before publishing any client name, logo, unreleased artwork, project details, image, or case study, confirm approval. The homepage currently proposed in the studio branch is **for review** and its partner names must be approved before production.

## Website

The public-facing website is intended to be simple, beautiful, fast, and useful: a studio identity, selected relationships or approved work, a concise sense of capabilities, and a clear contact path. It is **not** a newspaper, SaaS platform, AI interface, content dashboard, or personal operating system.

The current homepage redesign is on a focused review branch; do not assume changes on a branch are live.

Design principles:
- Cyber-white paper, near-black ink, restrained tangerine orange
- Sharp typographic hierarchy, fine rules, generous space
- A four-segment fruit mark as a small visual metaphor, not a complicated interface
- No gradients, rounded cards, unnecessary UI, or speculative case studies
- Responsive, accessible, static-by-default

Read [IDENTITY.md](IDENTITY.md) for earlier identity research. The work has moved toward a simple studio website, but the visual identity is still a design exploration, not a finalized trademark.

## Repository purpose

This repo holds technical work, reusable creative materials, design studies, and the history of earlier Tangerine/Haven experiments. It is **not** a client portal and should never contain private client documents, licensed source assets, credentials, or unreleased material without explicit authorization.

Historical experiments are retained deliberately:
- [SYNTHESIS.md](SYNTHESIS.md) — former AI synthesis concept
- [INSTRUMENT.md](INSTRUMENT.md), [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md), and [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) — earlier Haven/home-system studies
- Existing HTML prototypes, experiments, archives and feature branches

Those artifacts remain historical, **not** the present business direction.

## Working philosophy

Play long-term games with long-term people. Protect creative time. Keep the business small enough to give the work real attention. Prefer long-term relationships and aligned interests over transactional volume. Finish things beautifully and let them speak for themselves.
