08:00 Morning

On working between

Most problems worth solving don’t fit inside one discipline. A story needs an identity. An identity needs a system. A system needs a reason to exist — and a business strong enough to carry it. Tangerine works in the spaces between, so nothing important falls through them.

10:00 The practice

One fruit, six segments.

Every engagement draws on the whole practice. Some begin with a name, others with a painting, a product or a plan — but the segments always belong to the same fruit.

  1. Setting the frame that everything else lives inside. We define what a body of work should feel like, then hold every decision to it — from the first reference to the final proof.

    • Creative strategy
    • Art direction
    • Campaigns & launches
    • Editorial systems

  2. Original work made for its own sake, and commissioned work made with the same seriousness. Art keeps the rest of the practice honest: it is where we learn what we actually see.

    • Original work
    • Commissions
    • Image-making
    • Objects & editions

  3. Finding the true story and telling it in the right order. We write, edit and shape narratives that people can repeat in their own words — which is the only way a story travels.

    • Narrative & messaging
    • Writing & editing
    • Founder stories
    • Publishing

  4. Names, marks, typography and voice: the parts of a thing that people remember and recognise. We design identities as living systems, not logos in a drawer.

    • Naming
    • Visual identity
    • Typography
    • Voice & guidelines

  5. Websites, products and tools built with the same care as the brand they serve. We treat behaviour as part of the design: how something responds is how it feels.

    • Websites
    • Digital products
    • Interface design
    • Creative tools & AI

  6. Turning clarity into momentum. Positioning, offers and plans that let good work find its audience — and let the business behind it last long enough to keep making it.

    • Positioning
    • Offer design
    • Go-to-market
    • Advisory

12:00 Noon

Few, by design.

Tangerine works with a select number of clients at a time. Not as a policy of scarcity, but because that is how attention works. Every engagement is led by Cole directly — and we only begin when we can give the work what it deserves.

  • Led, not handed off.

    The person you meet in the first conversation is the person doing the work.

  • Close, not crowded.

    A short list means real availability, honest conversations and decisions made together.

  • Long, not loud.

    We prefer relationships that outlast a single launch. Good work gains meaning with time.

15:00 Method

The shape of a day.

An engagement moves like a working day: unhurried at the start, concentrated in the middle, reflective at the end. Then the hand comes back around to twelve.

00Twelve

  1. 01 · Two o’clock

    Listen

    We start with a conversation, not a proposal. What are you making, who is it for, and what would it mean to get it right?

  2. 02 · Four o’clock

    Find the story

    Underneath every brief is a truer one. We look for the story only you can tell — and the order it should be told in.

  3. 03 · Six o’clock

    Give it form

    Direction becomes something you can see and hold: names, images, words, type, artwork and objects.

  4. 04 · Eight o’clock

    Build the system

    One good idea becomes tools that hold up under use — identity systems, websites, products and the guidelines that keep them coherent.

  5. 05 · Ten o’clock

    Grow it

    Positioning, offers and plans that help the work reach the people it was made for, and sustain the business behind it.

  6. 06 · Twelve o’clock

    Return

    We come back. We look at what was made, keep what matters, refine what doesn’t, and help it age well. Return is as important as discovery.

17:40 Golden hour

What we hold to.

  1. I

    Attention over noise.

    Make space for the things worth noticing.

  2. II

    Originals matter.

    Keep authorship, history and context intact.

  3. III

    Design is the work.

    Material, typography, proportion and behaviour are not decoration.

  4. IV

    Return is as important as discovery.

    Good work gains meaning with time.

  5. V

    Make it worth keeping.

    Build with care, and for longevity.

19:30 Dusk

Led by Cole Calfee.

Tangerine is a practice, not an agency. Cole leads every engagement personally, moving between creative direction, art, storytelling, identity, technology and business as the work requires.

The most useful perspective is often the one that crosses a boundary — the artist in the strategy meeting, the strategist in the studio. Tangerine exists to keep those perspectives in one room, at one table, with you.

Who we work with

  • Founders shaping something new, who want it to feel considered from the first day.
  • Companies whose story has outgrown the identity that carries it.
  • Artists and makers turning a body of work into something that can last.
  • Teams bringing new technology to something deeply human.

Not sure you fit? Write anyway. The best work rarely arrives in a familiar shape.
