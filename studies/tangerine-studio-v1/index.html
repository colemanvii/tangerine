03 / About Tangerine

Small studio.
Big world.

Tangerine is an independent creative studio led by Cole Calfee, working at the intersection of art, storytelling, design, and business.

Sometimes that means directing a film. Sometimes building a visual identity, making a photograph, writing something worth reading, or helping a founder articulate a vision. The medium follows the idea—and the work is always personal.

In cyberspace as it is in heaven.
An organizing belief, not a slogan.
Made in Georgia · Working everywhere.
