Plate 1 · ConstructionThe grid

Everything is built from one unit: a square pixel that is either on or off. The same rules govern the wordmark, the symbols and the motion, so any piece can be redrawn by counting.

Plate 2 · WordmarkWordmark

Three cuts. The wide cut follows Industrial Study 004 onto the grid. The restrained cut is the companion text face. The script is the approved homepage wordmark, translated to one bit at falling resolutions.

Plate 3 · TypefaceAlphabet

Both cuts are drawn here by hand, pixel by pixel, so there is no font licence to check. Capitals, figures and the punctuation a label needs. The wide zero carries a centre dot so it never reads as O.

Plate 4 · SymbolSymbols

The identity brief asks for a symbol tested at 16, 32 and 64 px. Three candidates, each shown at actual size, then enlarged with its grid. The 16 px fruit is drawn by hand; the larger sizes are generated and then checked.

Plate 5 · MotionMotion

Bitmap motion moves in whole pixels and whole frames, so every piece here is stepped at 12–30 fps with no easing between cells. Each one shows how the mark is made, plays once when it scrolls into view and then rests. With reduced motion on, you see the final frame.

Plate 7 · FindingsFindings

What the grid shows about Tangerine's identity, and what to test next before any of it is used.

What works

  • The wide cut is a good fit for the grid. Industrial 004's broad, square proportion comes out of 2 px strokes naturally, so the wordmark feels drawn rather than converted.
  • At 96 px wide the script turns into something like stitched lettering. It stays warm and still reads as Tangerine.
  • The 16 px fruit is the most legible symbol at favicon size. Orange and a dark leaf are enough.
  • Motion that shows how the mark is built (plotting, resolving, reassembling) suits the material and says something true about the practice.

What doesn't, yet

  • The citrus wheel needs at least 32 px. At 16 px ten segments turn to noise, so that size is redrawn by hand with eight.
  • The signal T is distinctive but could belong to any industrial company. It needs Tangerine-specific evidence before going further.
  • Below 96 px the rasterised script loses its hairlines. A hand-cleaned 64 px version should be drawn and compared before using it small.
  • Diagonals in N, M, W and K step at 45° only, so W is the weakest glyph.

Next tests

  • Hand-clean the script at 96 × 38 and at 64 px, keeping the approved letterforms' entry and exit strokes.
  • Print the equipment label and the proposal page at 1:1 on white stock, then check the 1× restrained cut on paper.
  • Compare the wide cut against the vector Industrial 004 and the fruit symbol side by side before choosing.
  • Choose one motion, not seven. Plot or Resolve are the strongest candidates for a site loading state.
