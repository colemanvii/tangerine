03About Tangerine

Small studio.
Big world.

I’m a writer, painter, photographer, filmmaker, designer, and creative director from Georgia.

I work alongside ambitious founders, make things with friends, explore strange ideas, and pursue beauty wherever I find it.

Tangerine is where all of that lives. It is not an agency. It is one person with a lot of ideas, some extraordinarily talented collaborators, and an ambition to make beautiful things. The medium follows the idea—and the work is always personal. —Cole Calfee
