# TANGERINE / 2030
*A future study, written 16 November 2026. Not a forecast, contract, investment solicitation, or public client list.*

## The bet

Tangerine is a **small, independent, full-service creative and marketing studio**. Its breadth includes strategy, brand, design, typography, illustration, advertising, paid and organic social, PR, photography, film, and storytelling. Its business model is not fifty clients: it is a few committed, multi-year partnerships with aligned incentives.

The company must serve the life: two hours a day for personal music, time for painting and reading, the freedom to travel and build lasting friendships, and the ability to take real creative risks. The studio is not another dashboard, news startup, or AI SaaS company.

Our principle: **Long-term games with long-term people. Make great stuff. Put it into the world. Let the work speak.**

## Year-by-year goals

**2027 — Foundation / make it real.**

- Finish a beautifully restrained public studio site and secure permission for anything publicly attributed to collaborators.
- Publish three outstanding *approved* pieces of work, prioritizing one Freeman case study / film, one Kashus / Lil KC piece, and one Joseph Martin project.
- Put written agreements, usage rights, invoicing, cash reserves, tax planning, and project budgets behind all paid engagements.
- Build one repeatable content engine for Freeman rather than accepting undifferentiated requests.
- Make at least two hours of personal music a protected daily calendar block; quantify whether it actually happened.
- Target **$70k–$120k gross annual studio revenue**, not personal take-home. This is a planning range, contingent on contracted work—not a claim about current revenue.
- Establish three months of studio operating reserves before taking on payroll or speculative product inventory.

**2028 — Distinctive work / healthy systems.**

- Sustain 2–3 anchor relationships with clear weekly bandwidth and excellent output.
- Release at least four substantial works across different media and document the actual impact on clients.
- Bring a small trusted bench of freelance editors, shooters, illustrators, and specialists into individual projects; avoid permanent overhead until revenue is contracted.
- Negotiate any Lil KC IP participation transparently with counsel. A long partnership is a shared commitment, not an assumed ownership entitlement.
- Pilot Tangerine's own experimental work: music, paintings, a print, and a *non-infused* alcohol-free tangerine ale tasting.
- Target **$110k–$190k annual gross**, with margin and repeat business more important than volume.

**2029 — Earn the 2030 site.**

- Operate at a durable 3–4 **total** major partners at most; Scout, if ever won, must replace or meaningfully complement capacity, not force a sprawling agency.
- Have 6–10 truly excellent approved case studies, with photographs and documentary film that stand on their own.
- Maintain a sensible cash buffer and protect a 2-hour daily personal music practice, not just client music work.
- Establish at least one genuine, signed recurring license, production royalty, or other ownership-like participation—only if commercially and legally appropriate.
- Target **$160k–$275k annual gross**. Do not confuse revenue with net income or personal wealth.
- Reevaluate whether the owner wants to stay solo with collaborators or hire one full-time operator.

**2030 — The outcome, not the promise.**

The site should show four carefully chosen worlds **only if those relationships exist and are approved**; a small archive of work with deep editorial treatment; a tiny studio statement; an experiments section showing real music and paintings; and perhaps a real tangerine beverage. No fake logos, awards, testimonials, product drops, contracts, or published images. The site is successful if the work is undeniable and the company supports the life it was built to allow.

## Tangerine THC Ale — working concept, not a launch announcement

Creative proposition: tangerine peel, dry/bitter citrus, lovely bubbles, smart copy, an elegant can, conceived with Connor Blakely. The word **ale** should describe the bitter/citrus flavor experience, **not alcohol**, in any proposed THC product.

Start with a **zero-THC, zero-alcohol** formulation and a small private flavor test. Do not make manufacturing deposits, launch product pages, sell, distribute, select dose, or commission adult-use THC packaging until a specialist reviews federal and applicable state law, age gates, labeling, insurance, claims, lab testing, licensing, and retailer requirements.

As of November 2026, Georgia's hemp rules prohibit consumable hemp products in alcoholic beverages. Federal changes scheduled for December 11, 2026 would exclude final products over **0.4 mg total THC per container** from the federal hemp definition unless the law changes. Assess actual law at the time of launch, not assumptions from 2026. THC beverages are a **high-regulatory-risk speculative option**, not a core revenue line.

Kill criteria: if legal pathway, safe manufacturing, insurance, lawful distribution, or credible unit economics do not exist, pursue non-infused Tangerine Ale—or keep it as an art object.

## Scorecard — review quarterly

- **Freedom:** were 14 hours/week of private music protected? Are there weeks of genuine rest?
- **Work:** what three things shipped that you are proud to put your name on?
- **Relationships:** is each partner healthier and more trusting than 90 days ago?
- **Economics:** gross billings, collections, direct costs, owner pay, tax reserve, runway, unpaid time.
- **Originality:** did Tangerine make one thing no one commissioned?
- **Restraint:** what did we deliberately decline?

If money grows but the life gets smaller, revisit the model.
