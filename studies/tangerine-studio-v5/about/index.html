About.

Tangerine

A creative studio in Georgia, led by Cole Calfee.

The studio

Tangerine makes brands, and the things brands are made of: identities, advertising, illustration, photography, film, typography, art direction, websites and stories.

Alongside the commissioned work there are characters, paintings, experiments and notes. They come from the same place, so they live in the same place. Some of it is finished. Some of it is still becoming.

A change of address

ColeCalfee.com has moved in here. Everything Cole makes now lives under Tangerine.

Cole Calfee

Cole is a writer, painter, photographer, filmmaker, designer and creative director from Georgia.

He works alongside the people building things, makes things with friends, explores strange ideas, and pursues beauty wherever he finds it. Tangerine is where all of that lives.
