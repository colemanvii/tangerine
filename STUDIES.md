# Tangerine Studies
2026-11-25 · Working creative direction

## Place in Tangerine
Tangerine is a creative studio supporting founders, brands, and artists to express their heart in their art. Alongside commissioned work and ventures, Studies is an independent artistic practice: original digital printmaking, motion and interactive objects. It is not a new client service or a monetization obligation.

## Art direction
The starting reference is a vintage printed illustration of two tangerines with green leaves, orange bodies, red stippled shadows and cream paper. Translate its principles into original artwork, not a traced copy. Reference provenance and reproduction rights remain unverified; do not publish the reference itself without clearance.
- Organic, slightly irregular silhouettes; generous negative space.
- Limited inks: orange, oxblood, forest green, warm cream. Exact artwork ink values remain open for approval.
- Pixel stippling and dithering rather than smooth digital gradients; avoid generic game sprites.
- Start with 64, 96 or 128 pixel grids, integer scaling and crisp pixels; choose resolution for the artwork rather than enforcing one grid.
- One observable event per motion piece; restrained timing, no unnecessary camera movement, text or sound.
- Ordinary things, beautifully observed. Keep public descriptions simple.

## Production
Develop 001 as the master visual benchmark before scaling the collection. Aim for one excellent study per week, without a publishing quota. Preserve editable source, palette, pixel grid, timing, exports, references and approval history for each piece. Keep draft and approved states distinct. Use browser-native canvas or discrete bitmap frames for motion; export GIF or other formats when useful. Support reduced motion and replay. Test actual desktop and mobile rendering, navigation, both colorways and console errors before claiming a release is complete. Deployment READY is not visual verification.
