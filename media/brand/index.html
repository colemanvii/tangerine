05 / RULES OF THE ROAD
  1. Static by default. No auto-play, ambient animation, or moving backgrounds.
  2. Square edges, thin rules, strong grids, and generous space. No gradients or decorative cards.
  3. Readable body typography before clever display typography.
  4. Clearly separate journalism, original analysis, opinion, and demonstration content.
  5. Original illustrations and properly licensed photographs only.
  6. Orange is a signal, not wallpaper. Use it sparingly.
