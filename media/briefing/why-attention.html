OUR REASON FOR BEING

A more beautiful way to pay attention.

The world does not suffer from a shortage of information. It suffers from a shortage of attention worth giving.

There is an odd emptiness that comes from knowing what happened everywhere and having truly encountered almost nothing. We read the headlines, refresh the timeline, learn the latest name, and move on. By evening the whole thing has become a blur.

But the world is not a blur. It is astonishingly particular. Someone is building a room that will change the way people gather. Someone is designing a machine that will change an industry. Someone is writing a book we will not understand until we have lived a little longer. Someone has something to say that might stay with us for years.

There should be a place for that kind of attention. A place where the urgency of the news does not crowd out the beauty of the world.

Why a newspaper?

Because a newspaper is an invitation. It does not demand that you keep scrolling. It says: here is an edition. Here are the stories we believe deserve your time. There is a beginning, an end, and perhaps something to carry with you after you close it.

We love the energy of a good conversation, the pleasure of an unexpected discovery, and the authority of careful reporting. We want to bring them together without turning them into another endless feed.

What Tangerine hopes to become.

A daily briefing you look forward to receiving. Conversations that reveal something about the person behind the work. Films that make you notice ordinary places differently. A record of the fascinating, difficult, hopeful business of being alive in this century.

We will keep asking whether a story is true, whether it matters, and whether we have presented it beautifully. Not because beauty makes a claim more credible. It doesn't. But because clarity, proportion, and care are part of how we respect a reader.

We are making this for the person who wants to know what is happening without losing the desire to look around.

Pay attention. It's worth it.

— TANGERINE
