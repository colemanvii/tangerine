THE COMPANY / THE POINT OF VIEW

Curiosity
is a practice.

Tangerine is an independent, founder-led media company interested in the world as it is—and in how extraordinary it might be when we pay closer attention.

01 / THE MISSION

Make the world more interesting, intelligible, and beautiful.

We are fascinated by the people who are building the future and the people who make life worth living in the present. Technology and enterprise belong in the same conversation as literature, design, architecture, craft, and film.

Our model borrows the pace and personality of independent daily broadcasting, then takes a different editorial path: elegant reading, original visual work, generous curiosity, and a distinctly human point of view.

We don't want to keep a reader inside an app. We want them to go back into the world with better questions.

02 / THE ETHOS

In cyberspace as it is in heaven.

This is a design conviction, not a claim of perfection: the digital world should have room for order, beauty, harmony, delight, and intention.

It should be possible to make media with ambition without treating human attention as something to capture and exhaust.

03 / OPEN BY DESIGN

Transparent work. Independent judgment.

Our ambition is to develop an open-source publishing platform: reusable static templates, visible corrections, linked reporting, and a clear distinction between verified facts and analysis.

Open source does not mean that every photograph, interview, or original story is free to reproduce. The software and the editorial rights are separate. Any licensing will be explicit.

The first version is intentionally simple: real HTML, real CSS, no hidden feed, and no make-believe subscriptions or automation.

04 / NONNEGOTIABLESHOW THE WORK SHOULD FEEL
01

True.

Verify sources. Name uncertainties. Correct the record. An attractive lie is still a lie.

02

Alive.

Fewer pieces, better chosen. Interest, discovery, and surprise over constant stimulation.

03

Beautiful.

Type that respects reading. Images that matter. Good proportions. Nothing gratuitous.

THE INVITATION

The world is interesting.
Let's pay attention.
