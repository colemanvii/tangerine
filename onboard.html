Private beta / context import

Bring your context with you.

Haven does not need your entire conversation history. It needs a careful, reviewable model of what matters now: projects, people, commitments, questions, values and active threads.

This pilot does not connect directly to ChatGPT. Your profile stays in this browser unless you choose to copy it somewhere else. The goal is to test whether your existing ChatGPT context can become a useful Haven.

01 / Ask your ChatGPT

Let the account that knows you make the first draft.

Copy this prompt.

Open the ChatGPT account you use most. Paste this as a new message. Read what it produces before bringing anything back.

The prompt deliberately asks ChatGPT not to guess, not to psychoanalyze you, and not to include highly sensitive information. You remain the editor.

02 / Review + import

Paste the profile. Nothing is accepted silently.

Haven will validate the JSON and turn it into a local preview. You can delete it from this browser at any time.

03 / Your Haven

For this pilot, Haven is proving the reduction layer: can a messy history resolve into a calm, recognizable state? Calendar, email and live sync come after that is worth trusting.
