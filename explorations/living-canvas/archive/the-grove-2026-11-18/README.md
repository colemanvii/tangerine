# The Grove — archived study

Archived 18 November 2026 at the owner's direction: "too decorative, literal and illustrative." Development stopped; kept for the record.

A frozen, self-contained snapshot of `the-grove.html` as reviewed (commit `da498f3`), with its own copies of `shared.js` and `shared.css` so later work on the live studies can't change it. Fonts, `work.json` and media are read from `../../`.

Cut-paper citrus branches printed as three overprinting inks on Canvas 2D, regrown on each visit and moving in a breeze. `?study=1…7` and `?hour=` still work.
